01 / THE CHALLENGE

When roads are disrupted,
every route matters.

Disaster situations can make transportation and emergency supply delivery difficult, especially in regions with challenging terrain and limited road connectivity.

01
⌁

Geographical challenges

Hilly terrain, remote settlements and complex road networks can make transportation difficult.

02
⚠

Road disruptions

Floods, landslides and other disruptions may affect road accessibility and route reliability.

03
◷

Delayed information

Incomplete or delayed field updates can make it harder to understand current road conditions.

04
◇

Supply coordination

Emergency supplies need to be prioritised and directed towards the locations that need them most.

THE CORE QUESTION

How can we make emergency transportation decisions more informed, connected and efficient?

02 / OUR PROPOSED SOLUTION

Introducing NEXUS.

NEXUS is a proposed intelligent disaster logistics support system that combines road network analysis, risk assessment, disruption reporting and emergency supply prioritisation.

✳

One connected system.
Multiple intelligent decisions.

Instead of treating route selection, road risks and emergency supply distribution as separate tasks, NEXUS brings them together in a unified platform.

05 / TECHNICAL APPROACH

The technology
behind the intelligence.

NEXUS combines established algorithms and rule-based decision systems.

Component Technical approach Purpose
Road Connectivity Graph Represent locations and road connections.
Route Optimisation Dijkstra Find the lowest-cost route.
Risk Assessment Deterministic rules Assign risk scores to road segments.
Disruption Alerts Rule engine Generate warnings from reported conditions.
Delivery Prioritisation Ranking logic Order supplies by urgency.
District Dashboard Data visualisation Summarise connectivity and disruptions.
CORE ALGORITHM

Dijkstra's shortest-path algorithm

Dijkstra's algorithm explores a weighted graph and finds the minimum-cost path from a starting node to a destination, provided the edge weights are non-negative.

RISK MODEL

Road risk can be translated into an additional route cost. A blocked road can be excluded from route calculation, while a risky road can receive a higher cost. This is a decision-support model, not a guarantee of real-world safety.
